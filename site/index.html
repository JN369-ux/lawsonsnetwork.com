How we check who joins

Everyone in the network is checked properly.

Independence only works if you can trust the people around you. Every adviser firm goes through the same gate, and nothing is switched on until it has been passed.

  1. 1

    Introduction

    We learn about your firm, its principals and how you work today.

  2. 2

    Background and standing

    Identity, professional background and standing of the firm and the people who run it.

  3. 3

    Agreement

    Terms agreed and signed before any access is given.

  4. Approval, recorded

    A recorded decision to admit. Until it exists, no systems, provider routes or brand assets are released.

  5. 5

    Ongoing review

    Once live, files are reviewed on a regular, risk-based rhythm.
